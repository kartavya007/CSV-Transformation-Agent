Requirements ----


Requirement ID--Business Requirement--Transformation Rule--Output Field
BR-01--Only successful sales should be included.--Keep records where order_status = Completed.--order_status
BR-02--Cancelled and returned orders should not contribute to revenue.--Exclude Cancelled and Returned records.--order_status
BR-03--Calculate gross line amount.--quantity * unit_price.--gross_amount
BR-04--Calculate discount amount.--gross_amount * discount_pct / 100.--discount_amount
BR-05--Calculate net sales.--gross_amount - discount_amount.--net_sales
BR-06--Classify order value.--High >= 10000; Medium >= 5000 and <10000; Low <5000.--order_value_segment
BR-07--Normalize payment methods.--Credit Card -> CARD; Debit Card -> CARD; UPI -> UPI; Cash -> CASH.--payment_group
BR-08--Create customer region.--South: Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala. West: Gujarat, Maharashtra. North: Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan. East: West Bengal.--region
BR-09--Create delivery days.--If ship_date exists, ship_date - order_date; otherwise null.--delivery_days
BR-10--Flag delayed deliveries.--delivery_days > 3 => Delayed; otherwise On Time; missing ship date => Not Shipped.--delivery_status
BR-11--Standardize customer name.--Trim spaces and convert to title case.--customer_name
BR-12--Standardize email.--Trim spaces and convert to lowercase.--customer_email
BR-13--Create reporting month.--YYYY-MM derived from order_date.--reporting_month
BR-14--Create final output key.--Concatenate region + '-' + product_category + '-' + reporting_month.--reporting_key
BR-15--Output should contain only business-ready fields.--Do not include raw calculation helper columns unless needed for audit.--See Output_Schema
